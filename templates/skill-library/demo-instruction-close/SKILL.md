## Reconcile the final check

Inspect the latest approved checker packet and the exact candidate it evaluated. Do not rerun
tests merely to compose this document or present old repair evidence as a new final check.
<!-- sflow-instruction: demo-fresh-test-evidence -->

## Retain the closing documents

Summarize approved scope, final revision, scenario evidence, repair history, residual risks and
operational handoff. If no repair was needed, record the returned applicability decision.
<!-- sflow-instruction: demo-reviewed-routing -->

Only an authorized human closing approval ends the Story; authoring a report is not acceptance.
