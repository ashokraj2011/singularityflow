# Phase 5/7 coverage and remaining owners

This ledger makes the revised completion scope explicit. A connected registry operation is not
execution qualification. A configured requirement cannot be waived merely because its port is
missing. Historical delivery counts remain in their original notes; they are not fresh test
results for this change.

These rows remain mandatory under the revised specification. They are not silently reassigned to
Phase 8/9 or relabelled as optional just because the current reference workflows do not enable
them. Both Phases 5 and 7 remain open while mandatory rows are open.

Native terminal plan ceremonies, visual-profile decisions and native test-narrowing retain their
actual native contracts. Portable plan-row, witness and selected-recovery operations have separate
explicit contracts; they are not aliases that may borrow another operation's approval semantics.
Optional check, repair and data execution are unbound unless their corresponding execution or
retained-only capability is supplied.

Phase 8 authority migration and Phase 9 production host/distribution cutover remain separately
pending. No existing Git project has been migrated by these SDK-preview deliveries.
