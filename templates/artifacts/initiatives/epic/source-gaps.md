# Source Gaps and Questions — {{workId}}

A gap is anything the pinned sources do not settle but the Epic needs in order to
be specified. Recording a gap is not a failure — silently guessing past one is.
Every gap here must be closed, accepted with a named owner, or escalated before
the requirements phase can be approved.
