Every completed story can mint an Impact Receipt: velocity, quality, AI exposure, cost, governance counters, and a completeness grade — deterministic, bound to the completion commit, reproducible via `impact verify`. Studies are pre-registered in `singularity/impact.yml` (pinned at enrollment; complexity bands human-confirmed before implementation). `impact compare` matches cohorts and reports either a validated, quality-gated delivery gain (all guardrails held) or observed acceleration (faster, full stop) — the labels never blur. Every number carries sample sizes, method, evidence grade A–D, completeness, and confidence interval; missing data reads `unavailable`, never estimated; cohorts below the privacy floor refuse to render; there are no individual rankings, by design.
