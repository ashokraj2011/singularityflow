# The four places

The SFlow icon in the activity bar opens on four places:

- **Work**: say what you want done and press **Start**. Your tasks in flight are listed with their plans.
- **Needs you**: everything waiting on you, across the workspace's repositories. Open one to read it and decide.
- **Rules**: your team's rules for tasks. A rule only adds steps, and a new rule is proposed for review.
- **Library**: skills and agents, this repository's own and those of the marketplaces you trust.

**Advanced**, the last row under every place and a button in the view's title, shows today's Navigator with every
other screen. Every command stays in the Command Palette, with the same name.

Prefer today's Navigator first? Choose **Switch back** on the one-line notice, or
[choose it in Settings](command:workbench.action.openSettings?%22singularityFlow.navigation.layout%22): the four
places are then one row away.
