A v12 Story is refused by a build that knows only v11. That is how every schema step in this registry behaves: everyone on a team must upgrade before one of them starts a Story. The v12 fields add no authority. A decision is pinned with the Story's resolution like every other policy, and only when the workflow declares one, so an existing workflow's resolution and policy digest are byte-identical. A rule may send work back, or skip a phase a person signs off, only after a phase a person signs off (`[SPK:CON-037]`); a loop stops for a person at its round limit; a person's choice requires membership in the decision's pinned approval groups, a reason, and the exact pending-decision key, and is recorded as the new governing `decision-made` lifecycle event. Rework a decision chooses opens a change request and invalidates approvals exactly as a rejection does. All digests were computed from the file bytes, not copied from a failing assertion.
