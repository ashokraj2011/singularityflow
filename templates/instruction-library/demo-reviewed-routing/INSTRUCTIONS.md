description: Separates evidence-backed automatic route proposals from human approval and bounds repair/retest loops.
metadata:
  sflow-label: Demo reviewed routing
---

Propose pass only when every required scenario has fresh passing evidence. A demonstrated defect
proposes repair with failed clause identities, root cause, allowed paths and regression cases.
Unavailable access or inconclusive evidence proposes blocked, not an invented product defect.
Submit the configured decision value and follow the kernel-returned route. Humans still approve
the evidence and transition; disagreement returns work for correction. Every repaired revision
must return to the independent checker. Changed scope returns to intake. At the three-round bound,
preserve work and request renewed human direction; never waive a gate or manufacture success.
