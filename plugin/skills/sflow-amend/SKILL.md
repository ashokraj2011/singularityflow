This is the dedicated route for `singularity-flow story intent-amendment`; `/sf-reject intent-amendment propose` runs the same steps. Never edit approved intent in place, and never use phase rejection for it.

1. Inspect `singularity-flow status --for-agent --json` and `singularity-flow story intent-amendment status --work-id <WORK-ID> --json`. Confirm the person's changed intent and reason.
2. Run `singularity-flow story intent-amendment draft --work-id <WORK-ID> --clause <CLAUSE-ID>` (one `--clause` per changed clause). Write only the changed clauses in the file it issues.
3. Show the `summary` of `singularity-flow story intent-amendment preview --work-id <WORK-ID> --for-agent --json` in plain words (what changes, its size and why, what is redone or kept, who decides) and, in the same response, `ask_user` "Propose it?" (Yes, No), never as chat text. It records nothing.
4. Propose only on their Yes, in this same turn, and only when `singularity-flow nextsteps --for-agent --json` returns the `singularity-flow story intent-amendment propose` action: run `singularity-flow story intent-amendment propose --work-id <WORK-ID> --reason "<THEIR WORDS>" --authored governed-agent --channel copilot-host --json`. Do not invent an `update-intent` convergence finding or change YAML. Otherwise record the change in clarification and revise the scope draft.
5. Show the amendment ID, diff and blast radius. The decision and the acknowledgement are a person's, never this turn's: a person who approves the scope decides it on the decisions card, `/sf-decide <WORK-ID>` (Copilot CLI: `/decide <WORK-ID>`; VS Code: Singularity Flow: Decide Everything This Step Waits On), or in their own terminal, and the step it was proposed from pauses until then. They may reject and replace a stale proposal without changing approved intent. Anyone on the Story then acknowledges an approved one on the same card or in their own terminal. Never decide or acknowledge here; waive no tests or approvals. Closed Stories need reviewed reopening.
