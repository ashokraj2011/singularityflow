# Epic Intake Summary — {{workId}}

The intake summary is the Epic charter: why this work exists, who it is for, what
is in and out, and whether the evidence is strong enough to specify requirements.
It is the single input the requirements phase consumes, so anything asserted here
must be traceable to a pinned source in the source catalog.
